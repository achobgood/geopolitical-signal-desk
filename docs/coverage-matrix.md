# Collection coverage and access matrix

Documentation checked **November 3, 2026**. This is a provider inventory and authorization record, not a claim that every category is operational. Runtime collection history is the authority for successful executions. A successful HTTP fetch does not establish complete coverage, original reporting, source independence, or early-warning performance.

The required product is public-observation discovery followed by provenance-preserving correlation and assessment. An expanding set of mainstream news feeds is not a substitute for discovering low-engagement firsthand reports. Independent confirmation is not a collection requirement. Confidential sources, restricted groups, personal profiling, service-member identities, and sensitive current military positions/routes/times are outside scope.

## Status at framework implementation

- **Previously live:** Federal Reserve press RSS, used as an ingestion proof of concept. Its 20 initial records are economic official notices, not a geopolitical investigation dataset.
- **Public access verified in this build; shared adapters being implemented:** Mastodon public Iran hashtag timeline, UN News Middle East RSS, and CISA's structured KEV JSON catalog. The build's final execution report must supply actual run IDs and persistence results before these are labeled live.
- **Not connected:** X, Telegram, Reddit, Meta, maritime, aviation, market-price APIs, and broader local-language/community discovery. No credentials, paid subscriptions, approvals, or historical access are assumed.
- **Scheduling:** A sleeping Render Free web process is not a scheduler. Record scheduling configuration, actual dispatch, API response, collection-run completion, assessment outcome and notification outcome separately. Keep the existing ChatGPT hourly watch unchanged until its replacement passes that complete chain.

## Shared collector acceptance contract

Each provider must declare its category, access basis, authorization state, configured endpoints, credentials required, permitted retention, polling/freshness expectation, historical limits and whether it can expose original observations. Disabled/blocked providers remain visible in coverage reporting; they must not silently disappear or report successful zero-result coverage.

All implemented providers must produce the same normalized intake contract rather than custom table writes. Preserve original permalink, source platform/account **type** without unnecessary private identifiers, source language, publication time, event-time uncertainty, access/media-inspection status, original/repost/unknown status, attribution/origin references and collection limitations. Stable source identity drives idempotency; exact duplicate intake must not erase earlier evidence. Revisions, corrections and inaccessible/deleted records require a traceable reconciliation policy consistent with the provider's terms.

Collection eligibility, evidence analysis and notification eligibility are separate gates. A single unverified allegation may be collected or investigated. Repetition, low engagement, official status and agreement among derivative articles do not establish independence. Correlation must distinguish exact identity, shared origin, topical relationship, explicit support and contradiction. Novelty or severity scores must remain unassessed unless their method and evidence exist.

## Incremental cross-section and priorities

1. Exercise one common pipeline across **public social discovery metadata + official geopolitical material + a structured cyber source**. This is a useful technical cross-section, not comprehensive operational coverage. Keep the Federal Reserve demonstration separate from genuine geopolitical evidence.
2. Trace a narrow genuine scenario back to inspected originals, persist same-origin/related relationships, and save a low-confidence assessment with alternatives and falsification criteria. An unrelated cyber item must not be added to that scenario just to make the source count look diverse.
3. Resolve **social authorization** before adapter expansion: written X use-case approval and a cost cap; Telegram's explicit permitted-use/consent basis; Reddit approval/OAuth plus deletion reconciliation; Meta eligibility and a permitted grant. Do not ask for or purchase credentials as if they solve the licensing gate.
4. Add language/community query packs and a validated aggregate infrastructure/energy signal. Then validate delayed civil maritime/aviation aggregates against their own baselines. Prefer useful category coverage to completing a news catalog.
5. Verify unattended execution with durable dispatch/run receipts and failures. A cron entry or manually invoked job is configuration/test evidence, not proof of an unattended run. Full replacement acceptance additionally requires observed assessment and notification behavior. Leave **Geopolitical Escalation Watch II** unchanged until that proof exists.

No paid access or new external account was purchased/provisioned for this inventory. Numeric limits above are documentation snapshots; adapters must obey current response headers and their actual grants. Authorization-blocked and unimplemented rows are explicit remaining gaps, not promises of available coverage.
