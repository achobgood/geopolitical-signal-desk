Quantitative volatility analysis

MATHEMATICAL INDICATORS · COMPLETED DAILY/WEEKLY BARS
Deterministic SMA20/50, Wilder RSI14/ATR14 and confirmed daily/weekly reversal zones from Cboe source prices. VIX and VXN are volatility indices, not stocks. Their support/resistance is research context, never a BUY/SELL recommendation or a prediction of equity direction.

Forward-observed index outcomes

ONLY AFTER FIVE ACTUALLY COLLECTED FUTURE SESSIONS
These outcomes measure changes in the VIX/VXN *volatility index* after an earlier, time-stamped quantitative analysis. They are not investment returns, trade wins, or an estimate of predictive accuracy. No outcome can exist until a later official source series is collected.

Historical volatility event study

REAL CBOE DAILY BARS · RETROSPECTIVE, NOT HISTORICAL ALERTS
The 20% five-session volatility threshold is an uncalibrated attention rule. All sample windows are reconstructed from a dataset collected later. Event windows overlap and are not statistically independent. These are future changes in VIX/VXN index values, NOT stock returns, trade win rates, or pre-crash predictions.

Persisted market watches

ACTUAL DETECTION · NOT RETROSPECTIVE PREDICTIONS
These are simple daily-data threshold detections. They cannot identify the bottom, calculate a trade, or prove advance warning. Compare source collection and first-detected timestamps before evaluating any claim of early warning.
